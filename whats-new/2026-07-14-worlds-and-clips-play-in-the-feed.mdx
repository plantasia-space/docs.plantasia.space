## Worlds and clips play in the feed

A world or a clip in the feed now plays right on its card. A tap starts it in place, with its own loading and playback controls, and nothing opens on top.

## A home feed you choose

The home feed has four modes behind a switcher in its corner: a blend, the newest first, what people are playing, and older work you may have missed. It runs on counts the platform already had, with no new tracking behind it.

[Read about the home feed →](/docs/plantasia-space/home-feed)

## A play queue

Line up audios in the mini player and they play one after another, and the sound keeps going while you move around the site.
