## Worlds and clips play in the feed

A world or a clip in the feed plays right on its card, with nothing opening on top.

## A home feed you choose

Pick how the home feed is ordered: a blend, the newest first, what people are playing, or older work you may have missed.

[Read about the home feed →](/docs/plantasia-space/home-feed)

## A play queue

Line up audios in the mini player and they keep playing while you move around the site.
