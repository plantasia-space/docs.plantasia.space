## Three new instruments

![The Sampler in the Studio: the screen of its zones, then Sample, Amp envelope, LFO and Mod wheel.](/img/orbiters/instruments/sampler.jpg)

An Orbiter can now be played as an instrument. The **Sampler** (Rhizome) plays sample libraries: pick an instrument, set where each sample starts, ends and loops, and shape it with its envelope, LFO and mod wheel. **Sheaf** is a subtractive synth with one oscillator, a filter with its own envelope, an LFO and a mod wheel. **Granular** breaks an audio into grains and plays them back.

[Read about the Sampler →](/docs/orbiters/instruments/sampler)

## Up to three instruments in one Orbiter

![Picking an instrument for a World in a Notes Orbiter.](/img/orbiters/multiple-midi-instruments/notes-picker.jpg)

An Orbiter is now either Audio or Notes, and World I decides which. A Notes Orbiter plays up to three instruments side by side, one per World, each from its own keys and MIDI channel. An instrument with nothing feeding it notes gets a **Piano Roll** to play and draw them in.

[Read about multiple instruments →](/docs/orbiters/instruments/multiple-midi-instruments)

## Audio effects

![The Reverb in the Studio.](/img/orbiters/audio-effects/reverb.jpg)

Every Orbiter has a rack of audio effects: **Reverb**, **Delay**, **Flanger**, **Chorus**, **Compressor**, **Saturator**, **Filters**, **Multimode Filter**, **Parametric EQ** and **Bitcrusher**. The **One** modules (One Reverb, One Echo, One Flanger, One Chorus, One Compressor, One Saturator) each fit an effect into a single knob. Added as an insert, an effect loads doing nothing, so the sound only changes once you turn it up.

[Read about audio effects →](/docs/orbiters/audio-effects)

## MIDI effects

![The Drum Sequencer in the Studio.](/img/orbiters/midi-effects/drum-sequencer.jpg)

Notes can be shaped before they reach an instrument. The **Drum Sequencer** programs beats on pads and launches them in time with the Orbiter. **Note Echo** repeats every note, **Velocity** reshapes how hard each one plays, and the **Arpeggiator**, **Ephemeris** (a step sequencer), **Scale** and **Pitch** cover the rest.

[Read about MIDI effects →](/docs/orbiters/midi-effects)

## Effects on a Moon, a mixer on the Star

A Moon can now carry effects of its own. It works as a send and return: what it returns is added to the World through the Star's mixer, where the Moon's level and a crossfade set how much you hear. The Star also shows up as the host star in the Entangled Worlds view.

## Presets

Save a module, a World body or a whole rack as a preset, load it into another Orbiter, and export or import it as a file.

## Your own audio files

Drop audio files from your computer into an Orbiter and play them without uploading them. They stay on this device, keep their artist and artwork, and get their tempo detected so they sync like hosted tracks. A file browser shows every file the device holds.

## Collections offline

Download a collection once and play it with no connection, on desktop. Downloaded collections are listed on the offline library page.
