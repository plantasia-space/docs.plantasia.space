## New instruments

![The Sampler in the Studio.](/img/orbiters/instruments/sampler.jpg)

An Orbiter can now be played as an instrument: the **Sampler** plays sample libraries, **Sheaf** is a subtractive synth, and **Granular** plays an audio as grains.

[Read about the Sampler →](/docs/orbiters/instruments/sampler)

## Up to three instruments in one Orbiter

![Picking an instrument for a World.](/img/orbiters/multiple-midi-instruments/notes-picker.jpg)

A Notes Orbiter plays up to three instruments side by side, one per World. An instrument with nothing feeding it notes gets a **Piano Roll**.

[Read about multiple instruments →](/docs/orbiters/instruments/multiple-midi-instruments)

## Audio effects

![The Reverb in the Studio.](/img/orbiters/audio-effects/reverb.jpg)

Reverb, Delay, Flanger, Chorus, a Pitch shifter, a Frequency shifter, Compressor, Saturator, filters, EQ and Bitcrusher, plus **One** versions that fit an effect into a single knob.

[Read about audio effects →](/docs/orbiters/audio-effects)

## MIDI effects

![The Drum Sequencer in the Studio.](/img/orbiters/midi-effects/drum-sequencer.jpg)

Shape notes before they reach an instrument: a Drum Sequencer, Note Echo, Velocity, an Arpeggiator, a step sequencer, Scale and Pitch.

[Read about MIDI effects →](/docs/orbiters/midi-effects)

## Presets

Save a module, a World or a whole rack as a preset, and load it into another Orbiter.

## Your own audio files

Play audio files from your computer in an Orbiter without uploading them.

## Collections offline

Download a collection and play it with no connection, on desktop.
