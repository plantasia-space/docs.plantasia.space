## Rooms in the Playground

Open a room in the Playground and pass its code to a friend, and you play together, with up to four Orbiters between you. If nobody's around, Quick play finds someone who is also looking.

## Two people on one Orbiter

Two people can play the same Orbiter at once. A control you are holding shows as yours to the other player, and the whole session stays in sync.

## Sharing

Editors can manage who collaborates on what they edit, and you can get a notification when someone shares something with you.

[Read about sharing →](/docs/plantasia-space/sharing)
