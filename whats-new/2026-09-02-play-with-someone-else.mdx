## Rooms in the Playground

Open a room in the Playground and it hands you a code, something like bee-orchid-4821. Pass it to a friend, or share the picture it makes with the code on it, and they come straight in. A room seats two players, with up to four Orbiters between you. If nobody's around, Quick play looks for someone else who is looking and puts you both in the same room.

## Two people on one Orbiter

Two people can play the same Orbiter at the same time. A control belongs to whoever is holding it and shows as locked to the other player, with the name of the person holding it. The sensor toggle and the whole Cosmic LFO stay in sync between players, so both hear the same thing.

## Score rooms keep time

A room that follows a score has a section clock. The score moves the room through its sections and decides when control passes to a player. It can also pause the room, or leave it free.

## Sharing

Editors can manage who collaborates on what they edit, while General Access stays with the owner. You can get a notification when someone shares something with you.
