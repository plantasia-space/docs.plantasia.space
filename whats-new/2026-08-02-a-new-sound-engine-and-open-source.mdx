## A new sound engine

Orbiters run on a new sound engine. Stretch an audio without dragging its pitch along, play it backwards, or break it into grains. Every effect has its own visual behaviour in the scene, so what you hear has something to look at.

[Read the blog post →](/blog/instruments-you-can-open)

## Plantasia Space is open source

The code behind Plantasia Space is open, so you can read how the instruments you play are made.

[Read the blog post →](/blog/instruments-you-can-open)

## Versions and autosave

Release and edit forms for audios, worlds, Orbiters and collections save your work as you go, and releasing stays a separate step. An audio keeps its versions: open one in the form, save a new version or update the current one. Editing something released starts a draft, so what people hear doesn't change until you release again.
