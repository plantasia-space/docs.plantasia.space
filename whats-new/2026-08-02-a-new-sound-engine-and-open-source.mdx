## A new sound engine

Orbiters run on a new sound engine: stretch an audio without changing its pitch, play it backwards, or break it into grains. Each effect also shows in the scene.

## Open source

The code behind Plantasia Space is open, so you can see how the instruments you play are made.

[Read the blog post →](/blog/instruments-you-can-open)

## Versions and autosave

Your work saves as you go. Audios keep their versions, and editing something released starts a draft, so people keep hearing the released one until you release again.
