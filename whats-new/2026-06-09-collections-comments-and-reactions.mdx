## Collections

Build a collection that mixes audios, worlds and clips, and put its items in the order you want. Your public profile has a Collections tab, so anyone who visits can browse the collections you released.

[Read about collections →](/docs/collections/release)

## Comments and reactions

Leave a comment or a reaction on audios, worlds and the rest of the content on Plantasia Space. In account settings you choose which notifications reach you.

[Read about notifications →](/docs/plantasia-space/notifications)

## Clips in the home feed

Clips have their own tab in the home feed, and they show up in search. You can start a new clip from a world's page or from search.

[Read about the Studio →](/docs/entangled-worlds/studio)
