## What Orchidea is

Orchidea is the Plantasia Space assistant. You ask in plain words; it answers, finds things and, in a Studio, makes the change for you step by step. Its icon is the orchid.

## Who can use it

Orchidea comes with paid plans, and where you use it decides which: each place needs a plan of its own, shown below, read live from the same settings as the [pricing page](https://plantasia.space/pricing). Everyone can read these docs pages; only asking Orchidea is paid.

## What a request costs

Every request is paid with credits (see [Plans and credits](/docs/plantasia-space/plans-and-credits)). The price depends on the model you pick. The picker shows each model's price before you send and each answer shows what it was charged; a request never costs more than the price shown, and a request that never reached the model is refunded. ## What it can and can't do

- **On the site and in the docs**: it answers from these docs pages and links you to the right page. On the site it can also find things, read your stars, notifications and credits, and do small things on your own account: star an item, make a collection and add to it, add to your play queue, archive or restore your own items, and change your notification settings. It asks before archiving or changing settings, and you can undo what it did.
- **In a Studio**: it builds and changes the Orbiter or Clip you have open. Every request can be undone. Where a step costs credits beyond the request, such as a text written with AI in the Entangled World Studio, it asks first; turn off **Ask before using credits** in its **⋯** menu to let it go ahead in that conversation.
- It doesn't release anything, pay for anything or change other people's things.
- Its answers can be wrong; check what matters.

## Your conversations

Your conversations are private to you. In **Settings → Privacy → Orchidea conversations** you can stop keeping them, download them all or delete them all. How long they are kept is in the [privacy policy](https://plantasia.space/legal/privacy).
