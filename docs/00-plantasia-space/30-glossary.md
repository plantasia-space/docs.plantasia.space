The words used across Plantasia Space, from A to Z.

## A

### Acoustic community {#acoustic-community}
The species of a place that make sound, and together form the living part of its soundscape. Each community has its own signature in pitch and loudness, and it can be a sign of how rich a habitat is. Source: <a href="https://usir.salford.ac.uk/id/eprint/39080/13/BIO-D-15-00183R2%20cropped%20(3).pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Farina & James</a>.

### Aural community {#aural-community}
A community built around the listener as a creator: music and sound that keep changing, made between artists, listeners and the platform, so that each listen is its own. Source: <a href="https://archive.org/details/comunidad-aural-independent-v-1/mode/2up" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Comunidad Aural, Maar</a>.

### Axis {#axis}
One of an Orbiter's three performance controls: **X**, **Y** and **Z**. Moving an axis moves every control mapped to it, each between its own minimum, equilibrium and maximum. You move them on screen, by tilting a phone or with an LFO. [Map a control to an axis](/docs/orbiters/quickstart#map)

## B

### Body {#body}
One of the three parts of an Orbiter: the **World**, the **Star** and the **Moon**. Each has its own rack of modules, in up to three dimensions.

## C

### Certificate of authenticity {#certificate-of-authenticity}
A document that proves a work is genuine. For a digital work, it proves the copy is the real one.

## D

### Drawer {#drawer}
The panel in the Studio that shows a module's controls and its screen. It comes in several sizes; in the smallest, some screens hide and the knobs stay.

## E

### ENT (Entangled Token) {#ent}
An object that is physical and digital at once: a unique physical piece and a unique digital record (an NFT), linked by a certificate of authenticity. Each ENT supports a sister project that helps biodiversity or the wellbeing of the Earth's inhabitants.

### ENT Cards {#ent-cards}
Music released as a physical card and a digital release together, joining making, listening and owning music.

### ENT Worlds {#ent-worlds}
A network of connected sound planets and objects, between the digital and the physical world.

### Equilibrium {#equilibrium}
The value a mapped control takes while its axis rests in the middle, between its **minimum** and its **maximum**. Set it to the module's neutral value (Dry/wet 0 %, Amount 0) and a resting axis changes nothing.

## L

### Landscape {#landscape}
The word we use instead of "nature". In many cultures nature means everything that is not human; a landscape includes the people in it.

### Launch grid {#launch-grid}
The grid an Orbiter's starts, jumps and switches wait for, so that they land on a bar line. You pick it next to SYNC: **None**, 1 bar, 4 bars and so on. [Sync](/docs/orbiters/sync)

### LFO {#lfo}
One of an Orbiter's slow waves that move things by themselves, three per dimension (1cX … 3cZ). The axes and the modules share them: an LFO set once moves everything that reads it. [The Cosmic LFO](/docs/orbiters/interact#cosmic-lfo)

## M

### Moon {#moon}
An Orbiter body that works as a **send and return**: the World is sent to it, and it returns only its effect, which the Main mix adds to the dry sound. A reverb or a delay shared by the whole Orbiter lives here. [Three places for an effect](/docs/orbiters/audio-effects)

## N

### NFC {#nfc}
A short-range wireless link between devices. ENT Cards use it to store and read their digital information.

## O

### Orbiter {#orbiter}
A spaceship that is also a musical instrument: it plays a track or notes through its modules, and you play it with its axes, your phone or a MIDI controller. [Orbiters](/docs/orbiters)

## P

### Plantasia Space {#plantasia-space}
A platform for regenerative music. You publish sounds, place them in Entangled Worlds, and play them through Orbiters to explore and remake them.

### Play surface {#play-surface}
Where you play a Notes Orbiter by hand, in the Playback panel: the piano roll, and a tab for each generator in the chain (the Arpeggiator's keys, the Step sequencer's steps, the Drum sequencer's pads). It follows the dimension in focus.

## R

### Regenerative music {#regenerative-music}
Music published so that listeners can play it again in new forms. It draws on the cycles and frequencies of the landscape. Anything that sounds, released with intention, can be music.

### Room {#room}
A session that several people join to play the same Orbiters together, each on their own device. Everyone in a room shares one tempo and hears the same notes. [Sync](/docs/orbiters/sync)

## S

### Soundscape {#soundscape}
The sound of a place as people hear and understand it. It can be a real place or an imagined one, like a composition. Source: <a href="https://www.sfu.ca/sonic-studio-webdav/handbook/Soundscape.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Truax, Handbook for Acoustic Ecology</a>.

### Star {#star}
The Orbiter body on the main bus: after the **Main mix** blends the World and the Moon's return, the Star's effects play over everything. [Three places for an effect](/docs/orbiters/audio-effects)

## W

### World dimension {#world-dimension}
One of an Orbiter's three World chains, **I**, **II** and **III**, each with its own modules and its own way of mapping X, Y and Z. What World I plays (an audio, or notes) decides whether the Orbiter is an Audio or a Notes Orbiter. [Audio or Notes](/docs/orbiters/instruments)
