## Privacy settings

Open **Settings → Privacy**. There you find:

- **Orchidea conversations**: see below.
- **Help us improve Plantasia Space**: choose whether to share usage data.
- **Privacy & Cookies**: your cookie choices. See the [cookie guide](/docs/plantasia-space/cookie-guide).

Whether others can see your stars is in **Settings → Preferences** (**Show my Stars publicly**).

## Orchidea conversations

- Only you can see them. They are kept 90 days after you last use them.
- **Keep my Orchidea conversations**: turn it off and new conversations are not kept.
- **Download all** gives you every conversation as a file.
- **Delete all** deletes them in every app. Changes Orchidea made to your Orbiters and the credits spent stay.
- You may choose to share a conversation to help improve Orchidea. A shared copy is kept up to 12 months, and withdrawing deletes it.

## Deleting your account

Go to **Settings → Help → Danger zone → Delete account**. You have 30 days to change your mind and restore it. Your Orchidea conversations are deleted right away.

## The privacy policy

The full [privacy policy](https://plantasia.space/legal/privacy) says what we keep, why, and for how long.
