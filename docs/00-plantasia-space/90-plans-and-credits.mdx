## The plans

Each plan opens more of Plantasia Space: uploading tracks, Entangled Worlds, monthly AI credits, Orchidea and the Orbiter Studio. Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly.

The table is read live from the same settings as the [pricing page](https://plantasia.space/pricing), so it always shows the current plans:

## AI credits

All AI work is paid from one balance of credits: world texts, narration, textures, Orchidea and the Studios all spend the same credit.

<CreditFacts />

Your credits come from three places:

- **Plan credits**: a paid plan gives you credits every month. They reset when your month starts again; unused plan credits do not carry over.
- **Bonus credits**: credits we add for the month. They end with the month, like plan credits.
- **Bought credits**: credits from a pack. Each pack lasts 12 months from the day you buy it. Credits you bought before packs had an end date keep no end date.

When you need more, buy a pack in **Settings → Billing → Quick buy credits**:

## How credits are spent

Credits are spent in this order: your plan credits, then bonus credits, then bought credits, the ones that end soonest first.

Before you start an AI action you see what it costs. A texture costs a fixed number of credits that depends on the model you pick. For a text action, and for Orchidea, the price you see is the most it can cost: you are charged what it really used, and the rest comes back. Your balance, what each action cost, your purchases and when your bought credits end are on the [Credits page](https://plantasia.space/credits) and in **Settings → Billing**.
