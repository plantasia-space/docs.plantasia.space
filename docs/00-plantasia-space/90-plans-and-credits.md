## The plans

| Plan | Price | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Listener** | Free | Listen, follow, star and collect. |
| **Seed** | Paid | Also upload up to 5 tracks (200 MB per file). |
| **Bloom** | Paid | Unlimited tracks (200 MB per file), up to 7 Entangled Worlds, monthly AI credits and Orchidea. |
| **Flourish** | Paid | Everything in Bloom, files up to 300 MB, up to 70 worlds, the Orbiter Studio with up to 70 Orbiters, and more AI credits. |

Seed, Bloom and Flourish are paid **monthly** or **yearly**. Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://plantasia.space/pricing); they are shown before tax.

## AI credits

AI work is paid in credits, from two pools:

- **Text**: world texts, narration and Orchidea.
- **Texture**: images for worlds.

Each month your plan adds credits to both pools:

| Plan | Text | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Bloom | 50 | 7 |
| Flourish | 100 | 11 |

Plan credits end when your month resets. When you need more, buy a pack in **Settings → Billing → Quick buy credits**:

| Pack | Text | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Nano | 150 | 20 |
| Mega | 300 | 40 |

Pack credits are valid 12 months from the day you get them.

## How credits are spent

Credits are spent in this order: your plan's monthly credits, then the monthly bonus, then credits from packs.

Before you start an AI action you see what it costs. For a text action that is the most it can cost: you are charged what it really used, and the rest comes back. Your balance, what each action cost, and your purchases are on the [Credits page](https://plantasia.space/credits) and in **Settings → Billing**.
