## The playground

The [playground](https://plantasia.space/playground) is where you jam live with other people, each on
your own Orbiter, in one room. You need to be signed in. At the top you choose **Public jam** to join
someone, or **Create a jam** to open your own room.

## Join a public jam

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/plantasia-space/playground/public-jam.jpg"
  alt="The playground on Public jam: the length buttons 3, 7, 18 and 33 min, Enter code, and the Find a jam button."
  width={640}
  dots={[[1, 32.5, 22.1], [2, 55, 28], [3, 57.5, 59.1]]}
/>

1. Choose **Public jam**.
2. Choose the **lengths** you would play: 3, 7, 18 and 33 min all start on; turn off the ones you
   don't want (one always stays on).
3. Press **Find a jam** to join someone looking for a jam at one of those lengths.

**your momentum** is how much you have played lately, from 20 to 100; it fades if you stop.

## Create a jam

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/plantasia-space/playground/create-a-jam.jpg"
  alt="The playground on Create a jam: length 7 min, the collection search with suggested collections, and the score Layers."
  width={560}
  dots={[[1, 82.5, 11.3], [2, 55, 14.3], [3, 13, 20], [4, 9, 62.2]]}
/>

1. Choose **Create a jam**.
2. Pick a **length**.
3. Pick a **collection**: search for one, or choose a suggested one. Only audio and mixed
   collections with 5 or more released tracks can open a room, and the room shows what you, the
   host, can see.
4. Pick a **score** with the arrows on either side: it sets how the jam is shaped, for example who
   plays when. Its sections are described under it.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/plantasia-space/playground/jam-visibility.jpg"
  alt="Below the score: the visibility switch Also open as a public jam, the summary line, the Open a private jam button and Enter code."
  wide
  dots={[[5, 12.5, 6.5], [6, 58.5, 60.5]]}
/>

5. **Visibility**: turn on **Also open as a public jam** to list your room for people looking for a
   jam too. Your link keeps working.
6. Press **Open a private jam**. The line above it sums up your choices, and the button stays grey
   until you have picked a collection.

You can also press **Start room** on an audio collection's track list: it opens **Create a jam** with
that collection already picked.

## Rooms

- A room has a few seats for Orbiters and xPlorers; when they are taken, others can still listen.
- Everyone in a room sees the same things: what the room's host can see.
- In the waiting room, invite people with **copy the code**, **copy link** or **share room**. The host presses **start** when everyone is there.
- To join with a code, open the playground, choose **Enter code** and type it (for example `bee-orchid-4821`).
- A private room is open only to people with its link or code. A public room is also listed for people looking for a jam. Only the host can switch it.
