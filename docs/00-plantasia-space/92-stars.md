## Star something

Press **Star** on a track, Entangled World, Orbiter or collection. The button turns to **Starred**; press it again to remove the star.

## My Stars

Everything you starred is on **My Stars**: open **xPlorer** in the side bar and choose **My Stars**
(or go to `plantasia.space/<your username>/stars`).

Your stars are private unless you turn on **Show my Stars publicly** in **Settings → Preferences**.
Then others can browse them on your profile.

## Archive and restore

On your own items, **Archive** takes an item out of view without deleting it, and **Restore** brings it back. An item over your plan's limit can only be restored after an upgrade (see [Plans and credits](/docs/plantasia-space/plans-and-credits)).
