## Where to start

In the side bar or bottom bar, go to **Release → New Audio**.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/plantasia-space/release/release-menu.jpg"
  alt="The side bar with Release open: New Audio, New Entangled World, New Orbiter, New Collection and New Clip."
  width={360}
/>

The release form opens. It is one long page: go down it from top to bottom. The numbers in the
pictures below are the steps on this page. Your work is saved as a draft by itself while you fill it
in, so you can leave and come back.

## The audio and its information

<ModulePicture

### 1. Audio File

Choose an audio file in **WAV**, **AIFF**, **FLAC**, **OGG** or **MP3**. When it has been uploaded
and processed, the box says the audio is ready and you can play it there.

Every file is converted into a WAV (16-bit, 44.1 kHz) and an MP3, and those are what plays. An AIFF
can be heard once that conversion is done, because most browsers can't play AIFF directly.

### 2. Cover image

Upload a cover image for the Audio, or press the regenerate button at the top right of the cover to
get a public-domain picture; when you crop it you can choose whether it comes from plants, animals or
fungi. The cover is used on cards, previews and listings.

### 3. Audio Name and Artists

Give the Audio a name. If you need a starting point, the menu and the button just above the name
draw one from a plant, an animal or a fungus (**Plantae**, **Animalia** or **Fungi**).

Then add its **Artists**. You can add more than one artist, but they must already be on Plantasia
Space; people who are not can be credited in step 7.

### 4. Entangled World

Choose the Entangled World the Audio plays in by default. **Narration section** links the Audio to
one part of the World's narration (Introduction, Geography, Flora…), or keeps it tied to the whole
World.

### 5. Orbiter

Choose the Orbiter that plays the Audio by default. Only Audio Orbiters are offered: Orbiters whose
World I plays the audio, on the **Audio player** or **Granular**. A Notes Orbiter plays its own
instruments, so it can't play an Audio. See [Audio or Notes](/docs/orbiters/instruments).

The World and the Orbiter are only the defaults: listeners can still play the Audio with others.

## Details (optional)

**Review Details** is closed when the form opens. Open it to add more about the Audio; everything in
it is optional.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/audios/release/review-details.jpg"
  alt="Review Details open: Audio Metadata with the content type, Genre, Mood and Additional Tags, then Story & Credits with Description and Credits."
  width={600}
  dots={[[6, 57, 9.4], [7, 57, 69.6]]}
/>

### 6. Audio Metadata

Choose what the Audio mainly is (**Music**, **Spoken**, **Soundscape** or **Other**) and add a
**Genre**, a **Mood** and **Additional Tags**. Most of this shows on the Audio's card and helps
others understand the release.

### 7. Story & Credits

A **Description** tells the story of the Audio, and **Credits** names everyone else who worked on it
(producer, engineer…).

### 8. Audience

- **Private**: only you can open it. It is not released and not listed.
- **Shared**: anyone with the link can open it, but it is not listed publicly.
- **Public**: everyone can find it, in searches and public listings.
- **Schedule**: releases it to the audience you choose at a date and time you set. Scheduling is for
  Flourish xPlorers.

**Enable Direct Downloads** lets listeners download the Audio file. It is off until you turn it on.

### 9. Licence

Audios are released under **Creative Commons BY 4.0**: others can share and adapt the Audio if they
credit you. Turn on **Noncommercial** to make it **BY-NC 4.0**, which also rules out commercial use.

:::info
**You do not give up your rights.** Publishing an Audio under Creative Commons does not mean transferring your copyright. You remain the rights holder. By releasing it, you grant Plantasia Space a limited license to host and display your Audio, and you grant the public the permissions defined by the selected Creative Commons license (for example, listening, sharing, or remixing under its terms).
:::

:::warning
**AI and machine learning use.** Audios published on Plantasia Space are **not used for AI training, machine learning, or model development**. The platform does not currently request or collect consent for AI training because no models are being trained. Any audio analysis performed is purely technical (for example, descriptors or feature extraction). If this were to change in the future, you would be clearly informed and asked for explicit consent before any such use.
:::

### 10. Confirm and release

Turn on **I confirm that I own the rights to this content**: you can only release what you have the
rights to, or permission from its creator to publish. **Audio Readiness** below it counts what is
still missing; when it is complete, press **Release Audio** at the bottom of the form.

To change an Audio after release, open it again: release your edits as a **New version**, or
**Update current** to replace the live version without a new number.

## Audio quality when you listen

How Audios sound for you while you listen is a setting of yours, not of the release: **Settings →
Preferences**.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/audios/settings-audio-quality.jpg"
  alt="Settings, Preferences: Audio quality set to Mid and Audio buffer set to Auto."
  wide
/>

- **Audio quality** sets how much the player gives to sound against performance (Mid is
  recommended).
- **Audio buffer** follows the audio quality unless you change it; a change takes effect the next
  time an Orbiter opens.

Playback adapts to your connection: on a fast, steady connection it can stream the WAV; on a slower
one it falls back to the MP3 so the sound keeps going.
