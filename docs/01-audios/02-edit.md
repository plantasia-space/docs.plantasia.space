You can change an Audio after you release it: its information, its cover, where it plays, and who can
listen. Listeners keep hearing the version you released until you publish your edits.

## Open the editor

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/audios/edit/audio-menu-edit.jpg"
  alt="The top of the Audio Skysounds 3 VII with its menu open: Open in new window, Add to Collection, Share, Edit, Archive and Delete."
  wide
  dots={[[1, 97.3, 14.4], [2, 87.5, 65.7]]}
/>

1. On one of your Audios, open the **⋯** menu at the top right.
2. Choose **Edit**.

The Audio opens in the same form you released it with: see [Release](/docs/audios/release) for each
part of it. You can change its name and artists, its cover, its Entangled World and Orbiter, its
metadata, story and credits, and its audience and licence.

## Saving

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/audios/edit/edit-header.jpg"
  alt="The top of the edit form: Edit Skysounds 3 VII, the save icon with its dot, the version menu showing v1 Live, and the Version history open."
  wide
  dots={[[3, 83, 13.7], [4, 57.4, 35], [5, 50.5, 55.3]]}
/>

3. **Your edits save themselves.** A couple of seconds after you stop typing, your draft is saved;
   there is no save button to remember. The dot beside the disk icon changes colour as it goes:
   unsaved changes, saving, saved, or couldn't save. Hover it to read the state, or click it to save
   straight away. Audio files and cover images are the exception: they are applied when you upload
   them.
4. **The version menu** shows which version is in the form: **v1 Live** is the version listeners
   hear. As soon as you change something, the form becomes your **Draft**.
5. **Version history** lists every version. Tap one to load it into the form and edit from it; your
   current draft is kept.

## Publish your edits

Saving only updates your draft: nothing reaches listeners until you publish it, with the button at the
bottom of the form. Its menu chooses how:

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/audios/edit/release-as.jpg"
  alt="The Release as menu of the button at the bottom of the form: New version (v2), Publish your edits, v1 kept in history; Update current (v1), Replace live in place, no new number."
  width={320}
  dots={[[6, 37, 13.3]]}
/>

6. **Release as**:
   - **New version**: publishes your edits as the next version (v2); v1 stays in the history.
   - **Update current**: replaces the live version in place, with no new number.

   The button then reads **Release new version** or **Update current version**.
