You play one by moving three <Term id="axis">axes</Term>, X, Y and Z, on screen or by tilting a phone.

## What an Orbiter is made of

- **An instrument**: an audio you choose, or notes.
- **Effects** that shape its sound.
- **The axes** that move them: X, Y and Z, each mapped to the controls you pick.

<IsoFigure name="orbiters/saucer" label="An Orbiter drawn as its craft: a glass dome over a wide ring, with a belly and a hatch under it. Three tracks run round the ring for X, Y and Z, each with a light that rides it.">
  Move over the craft: across turns X, up and down turns Y.
</IsoFigure>

Think of an Orbiter as a small craft:

| On the craft | In an Orbiter |
|---|---|
| **The three tracks** round the ring | the axes X, Y and Z, from the outside in |
| **The light** on each track | where that axis is. It goes once round as the axis turns from −180° to 180°, and rests nearest you at 0° |
| **The lit part** of a track | how far the axis has turned, as on its knob |

Its console, with the knobs, the screen and the modules, is on [Interact](/docs/orbiters/interact).

When an Orbiter is open, you can:

- **Play and loop** what it plays
- **Move X, Y and Z** to transform the sound
- **Switch dimensions** (I, II and III): each has its own modules and its own mapping of X, Y and Z
- **Add movement** with the Cosmic LFO: slow waves, three per dimension, that the axes and the modules share, each moving what reads it by itself

[Interact](/docs/orbiters/interact) goes through each of these.

## What this section covers

- **[Quickstart](/docs/orbiters/quickstart)**: your first Orbiter in 5 minutes, from an empty Studio to playing it
- **[For DAW users](/docs/orbiters/for-daw-users)**: the World, the Moon and the Star as tracks, return tracks and the main bus
- **[Release](/docs/orbiters/release)**: create and publish a new Orbiter
- **[Edit](/docs/orbiters/edit)**: change an Orbiter you already made, and map its controls to the axes
- **[Interact](/docs/orbiters/interact)**: open an Orbiter and play it
- **[Sync](/docs/orbiters/sync)**: play several Orbiters together on one shared tempo
- **[Sensors](/docs/orbiters/sensors)**: play an Orbiter by tilting a phone

And the modules you build an Orbiter from, in four categories:

- **[Instruments](/docs/orbiters/instruments)**: what makes the sound, an audio you choose or notes
- **[Audio effects](/docs/orbiters/audio-effects)**: what shapes the sound
- **[MIDI effects](/docs/orbiters/midi-effects)**: what makes and reshapes the notes before the instrument plays them
- **[Visual modules](/docs/orbiters/visual-modules)**: what the sound does to the picture around the Orbiter
