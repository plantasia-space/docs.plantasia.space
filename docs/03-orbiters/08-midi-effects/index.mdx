**MIDI effects** work on notes, not on sound. They sit **before** the instrument, so they decide what
it plays: which notes, when, and how hard. They belong to a Notes Orbiter, whose instruments play
notes.

## Generators and MIDI effects

There are two kinds, and the order is always the same:

1. **A generator** makes notes on its own, so it leads the chain: the **Drum Sequencer**, the
   **Arpeggiator**, **Ephemeris**. A dimension has at most one.
2. **MIDI effects** reshape the notes that reach them, from a generator, the keys, MIDI hardware or
   the piano roll: **Note Echo**, **Chord**, **Velocity**.

Notes travel inside the audio stream, on the same clock as the sound, so an echo or a ratchet lands
exactly on the beat.

:::tip[Every MIDI effect loads neutral]

Adding one changes nothing until you move something: Note Echo and Chord load silent (Level at 0 %), Velocity loads as a straight line. Turn them up while you play.

:::
