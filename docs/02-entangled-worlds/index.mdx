## What a world holds

- **A real exoplanet**, with its sky, its star and the data astronomers measured
- **Its elementals**: geography, flora, fauna, society, history and elements
- **A story** in seven chapters, with a voice that reads it
- **A landscape**: the planet in 3D with its texture, clouds and moons, and a picture for each chapter
- **Audios** released inside it, and the **Orbiters** that play them

## The world page

A released world has its own page on Plantasia Space. The main page shows the planet and its moons; once the story has a voice, **Listen to the story** plays it all. Each chapter you write in World Studio becomes one part of the page:

<WorldParts caption="Each part shows its chapter's text, lit up line by line while the voice reads it, and the picture you pinned for it." />

Move between the parts with the links at the foot of each one.
