Editing an Entangled World opens it in **EW Builder**, the same four steps you released it with: **Origin**, **Story**, **Landscape** and **Release**. Go straight to the step you want to change.

## What you can edit

| To change | Go to |
|---|---|
| The exoplanet or the world's name | Origin |
| The elementals, the story's text or its voice | Story |
| The world in 3D, its texture, moons, clouds and light, or the sections' pictures | Landscape |
| The cover, collaborators and access, or the story as audio | Release |

Each step is described in [Release](./01-release.md).

## On a phone

EW Builder works on a phone. The top of the page reads **Step 2 of 4 · Story**: tap it to list the steps. The inspector opens as a sheet from the bottom, the drawer's cards scroll sideways, and Release is a single column.

## Iteration mindset

Entangled Worlds are designed to evolve.

You can revisit a world, regenerate its texture, rewrite its story, make new pictures or adjust its look as your ideas develop. **Undo** and **Redo**, at the top left, step back and forward through your changes.

## Saving and publishing changes

Your edits save themselves. A couple of seconds after you stop changing something, the world's draft is saved for you. There is no save button to remember.

The dot at the top of the page shows the state of that save.

Saving only ever updates your draft. Visitors keep seeing the version you last released, so you can regenerate textures and rewrite the story without anyone watching over your shoulder.

Select **Release Changes**, in the Release step, when you want your changes to apply to the public version of the world. That step is always yours to take.

Uploads are the exception: images you add are applied when you choose them, not by autosave.

## Archived worlds

An archived world can't be edited. Restore it on Plantasia Space to continue.
