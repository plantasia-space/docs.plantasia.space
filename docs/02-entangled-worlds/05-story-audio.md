Once your world's chapters have a voice, you can release the story as one Audio. The chapters you pick are joined in your order, with the world's cover, name and people. The Audio plays in Orbiters like any other, and counts toward your plan's number of Audios.

A chapter can be picked once its voice matches its text and its recording is ready. If you changed the text after the voice was made, the chapter shows **voice older than its text** (in World Studio's Story step it shows **Older than the text**). Make its speech again in World Studio to use it.

## From the world page

The world needs to be released first, and you need to be its owner or an editor.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/worlds/story-audio/world-menu.jpg"
  alt="The world page of Amagante de Gran Canaria with its Release menu open: New Audio, New Clip and The story as audio. Listen to the story, 5:41, is under the world."
  width={480}

/>

1. Open the world's **Release** menu and choose **The story as audio**. It shows once a chapter has a voice.
2. Tick the chapters and drag them into order.

<ModulePicture
     src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/worlds/story-audio/dialog.jpg"
     alt="The story as audio: Canaria · The story, the seven chapters with their length, Geography ticked, and Make the draft."
     width={420}
     dots={[[2, 3, 36.4], [3, 64.9, 90.6]]}
   />

3. Select **Make the draft**. The Audio form opens on the draft: check it and release it there.

Releasing it again later makes a new version of the same Audio.

## From World Studio

In the [Release step](./01-release.md#collaboration), turn on **Release the story as audio**, then pick the chapters and their order. Your choice is kept with the world's draft.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/worlds/story-audio/builder.jpg"
  alt="Story audio switched on in the Release step: Canaria · The story, 7 chapters, 5:50, with every chapter ticked, above World Readiness and Release Changes."
  width={300}
/>

Select **Release Entangled World** (or **Release Changes**): the world is released first, then the Audio form opens on the story's draft.

If a chapter shows **voice older than its text**, you can select it and confirm: the chapter's text goes back to the version the voice reads, and your newer text is replaced. You can undo this.
