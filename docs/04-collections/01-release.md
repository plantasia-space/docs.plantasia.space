## Where to start

Go to **Release → New Collection**.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/plantasia-space/release/release-menu.jpg"
  alt="The side bar with Release open: New Audio, New Entangled World, New Orbiter, New Collection and New Clip."
  width={360}
/>

The **Create collection** form opens. Fill it in from top to bottom; the numbers in the picture are
the steps below. The collection is saved as a draft by itself while you work on it (the save icon at
the top of the form shows it), so you can leave and come back.

### 1. Cover image

Click or drag to upload the collection artwork: PNG, JPEG or WebP, with a square crop. Or press the
regenerate button under it to get a public-domain picture of a plant, a fungus or an animal: the
**Plantae**, **Fungi** and **Animalia** buttons choose which.

### 2. Name

Give the collection a name. The button beside the name draws one for you if you need inspiration.

### 3. Description

Describe the collection: what it is, what inspired it, who worked on it.

### 4. Category and subtype

Choose the **Category**: **Audios**, **Worlds**, **Orbiters** or **Mixed**. The **Subtype** is
optional (for example Album, EP, Single, Compilation or Various Artists for audio collections).

### 5. Who can open it

A new collection is **Restricted**: only you and the xPlorers you add can open it, and it is not
listed or searchable.

- **Add xPlorers**: choose a role, then search for an xPlorer. A **Viewer** can open the collection,
  a **Publisher** can also publish it, and an **Editor** can also edit it and manage who works on it.
- **General Access**: switch **Restricted** to **Anyone with the link** to open the collection to
  everyone, with the role you choose (Viewer to start). Then it can also show in public listings and
  search. Only the owner can change General Access.

### 6. Release collection

Press **Release collection** when it is ready. After release, the collection no longer saves
by itself: publish your edits with **Release changes**.

## Add items

You can add content from inside the collection, or from anywhere on the platform: every audio, world,
and Orbiter page has an **Add to collection** action. Pick the target collection, or create a new one
on the spot, and optionally attach a note for collaborators.

Adding a different content type to a typed collection converts it to **Mixed**; you are asked to
confirm before the category changes.
