## Your collections

All your collections are on **My Collections**, in the xPlorer menu.

1. The tabs group them: **Released**, **Unreleased**, and **Archived** when you have archived any.

## Open the editor

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/collections/edit/collection-menu-edit.jpg"
  alt="The menu of the collection Hawkweed: Share, Edit, Archive and Delete."
  width={400}
  dots={[[2, 78.5, 13.6], [3, 78, 54]]}

2. Open the **⋯** menu of the collection.
3. Choose **Edit**.

<ModulePicture
  src="https://herbarium.plantasia.space/docs/en/media/collections/edit/edit-collection.jpg"
  alt="The Edit collection dialog of Hawkweed: cover image, Name, Description, Category, Subtype, Collaboration with General Access set to Anyone with the link, and the Release changes button."
  width={420}
  dots={[[4, 23.5, 2.5], [5, 61.5, 96.8]]}
/>

4. **Edit collection** has the same fields as when you created it: cover image, name, description,
   category and subtype, and who can open it. See [Release](/docs/collections/release) for each one.
5. On a released collection, press **Release changes** to publish your edits. Until you do, visitors
   keep seeing the collection as it was.

While a collection is still a draft, your edits save themselves: the small disk icon at the top of the
dialog shows it, and its dot changes colour as it goes (unsaved changes, saving, saved, or couldn't
save). Once a collection has been released it no longer saves by itself: your edits wait for
**Release changes**. Artwork is the exception: an image you upload is applied when you choose it.

## Reordering items

Collections can hold a mix of content types, and you can arrange the items in any sequence — the order you set is what visitors see on the public collection page.

Open the collection you want to curate and drag items into their new positions. The new order is saved as you go; there is no separate save step for reordering.

## Archived collections

An archived collection can't be edited. Restore it first to continue editing.
